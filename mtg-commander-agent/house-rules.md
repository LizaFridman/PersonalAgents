## Pod philosophy

- Core directive: **"the card does what the card says."**
- Encourage pillow-fort and defensive strategies.
- Strictly ban salt-inducing stax locks, infinite combos, fast-mana blowouts, and cheap/uninteractive win conditions.

## Global pod rules & heuristics

1. **Mana & acceleration**
   - Fast mana is banned, with the explicit exception of **Sol Ring**. Mana dorks, rituals, *Smothering Tithe*, and *Rhystic Study* are permitted.
2. **Win/loss conditions & pillow fort**
   - "Win the game" text is strictly banned across all cards.
   - "Player loses the game" text is banned — **except** *Ezio Auditore da Firenze*, which is explicitly allowed.
   - Pillow-fort support ("you can't lose the game," "opponents can't win the game") is permitted.
3. **Extra turns & deck blowouts**
   - Extra turns are capped at a maximum of 1 extra turn per round; any card granting 2 or more extra turns (e.g. *Time Stretch*) is strictly banned.
   - Single-card deck dumps that cast/cheat massive portions or all of a deck (e.g. *Omniscience*, *Primal Surge*, *Rooftop Storm*, *Dracogenesis*) are banned.
4. **Counterspells & stax**
   - Free (0-mana) counterspells are banned; non-counterspell free spells are permitted.
   - No mass land destruction, land bounce, or land-freezing effects.
5. **Poison / toxic / infect counters**
   - Capped at 9 poison counters max (corrupted threshold is 3).
   - Infect functions as Wither in combat, capped at 9 poison counters max.
6. **Combos**
   - No infinite loops or infinite combos may be **executed** in a game — this is a play-pattern restriction, not a deckbuilding exclusion. A card isn't cut from a deck just because it can combine with another specific card to form an infinite loop; both cards can be included as long as neither is individually on the Custom ban list below. The player simply doesn't chain the loop during an actual game — same trust-based norm as the deal-making guidance in `deckbuilding-framework.md`'s table-politics section.
   - Only treat a combo as an actual reason to cut cards if the deck's real game plan *is* the loop (i.e., it's the deck's intended win condition, not an incidental interaction between two otherwise independently-justified inclusions). See `deckbuilding-framework.md`'s Step 3 for the standalone-value question that's the real test for whether a combo piece earns its slot.

## Custom ban list

Specific cards to flag when validating decklists, beyond (or instead of) the official Commander banned list — a card banned here is a cut even if legal officially. If your group has ever *unbanned* something the official list still bans, don't list it here — house unbans of officially banned cards aren't something the agent should treat as legal.

- **Fast mana & lands**: Gaea's Cradle, Ancient Tomb, Growing Rites of Itlimoc, Storm the Vault
- **Stax & resource locks**: Blood Moon, Contamination, Grand Arbiter Augustin IV, Drannith Magistrate, Harbinger of the Seas, Tainted Aether
- **Multi-turns & deck dumps**: Primal Surge, Defense of the Heart, The One Ring, Time Stretch
- **Theft, discard & oppression**: Sen Triplets, Notion Thief, Sire of Insanity, Tergrid, God of Fright, Mindslaver, Gilded Drake
- **Combo enablers**: Isochron Scepter, Dockside Extortionist
- **Spells & efficiency**: Mana Drain, Force of Will, Orcish Bowmasters, Hinata, Dawn-Crowned
- **Burst & combat**: Blightsteel Colossus, Hatred, Brigid, Clachan's Heart

## Power-level agreement

Governed by the Pod Philosophy and Global Pod Rules above rather than the official Bracket system (`commander-rules-reference.md`) directly — a deck should read as pillow-fort/interactive-friendly, not stax-lock or infinite-combo-focused, regardless of what bracket it would otherwise sit at.
